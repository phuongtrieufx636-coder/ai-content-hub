1. What to Look For When Buying a Robot Vacuum Under $300

When you’re hunting for a budget robot vacuum, the key is to balance performance, smart features, and durability. Start with cleaning power—look for models with at least 2000–2500 Pa suction, which is enough to tackle pet hair and fine dust on hardwood and carpet. Next, consider navigation: laser‑guided or visual mapping systems provide more precise coverage than random‑walk models, especially in rooms with obstacles or multiple floors. Battery life matters too; aim for 90–120 minutes per charge so the unit can finish a full house in one go. Don’t forget dustbin capacity; a 0.5–0.7‑liter bin is standard for budget units, but larger bins mean fewer emptying stops. Finally, check the noise level—a quiet vacuum (≤55 dB) won’t disturb your daily routine or pets.
