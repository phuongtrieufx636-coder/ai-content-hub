1. What to Look for in Budget Wireless Earbuds

When hunting for affordable earbuds, don’t sacrifice quality for price. Key factors include audio performance, battery life, comfort, and connectivity stability.
- Audio: Look for earbuds that offer clear highs, balanced mids, and decent bass. Even budget models now feature hybrid drivers or adaptive EQ to deliver richer sound.
- Battery: A minimum of 4–6 hours per charge, plus a charging case that adds 10–15 more hours, is standard. Fast‑charge support (15‑minute boost for 3‑4 hours) is a huge plus.
- Comfort & Fit: Ear tips in multiple sizes, lightweight design, and a secure fit are essential for long listening sessions.
- Connectivity: Bluetooth 5.2 or newer ensures low latency and stable pairing. Check for multipoint support if you’ll switch between devices.
- Durability & Sweat Resistance: An IPX4 rating or better protects against sweat and light rain, extending the life of your earbuds.

Prioritizing these features ensures you get the best bang for your buck.
