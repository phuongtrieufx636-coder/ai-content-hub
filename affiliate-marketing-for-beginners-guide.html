Introduction

Affiliate marketing is a popular way to earn passive income by promoting products you love. Whether you’re a blogger, social media enthusiast, or just curious about online earning, this guide will walk you through the fundamentals and help you launch a successful affiliate strategy.

1. Understanding Affiliate Marketing Basics

Affiliate marketing is a performance‑based partnership between a merchant and a publisher. The merchant creates a product or service; the publisher—often a blogger, influencer, or website owner—promotes that product using unique tracking links. When a visitor clicks the link and completes a desired action (like making a purchase or signing up for a trial), the publisher earns a commission.

The beauty of this model lies in its simplicity: you don’t need to create your own product, hold inventory, or manage customer support. All you need is a platform to share content and a strategy to drive traffic. Most affiliate programs are free to join, and commissions can range from a few percent to several hundred dollars per sale, depending on the niche and product.
